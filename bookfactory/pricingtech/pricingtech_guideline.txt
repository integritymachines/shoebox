AUTHOR     Oliver Beige, PhD-level economist, game theorist and industrial engineer; startup, enterprise and academic expertise.

AUDIENCE   Founders of technology startups. Assumed: formal modeling in engineering contexts; economics at "Information Rules" level; scepticism toward academic economics for startups. Not assumed: empirical and econometric methods.

GOAL       Bridge academic economic analysis and founder reality: rigorous economic reasoning behind pricing decisions without the data richness academic models assume. Sophistication above MBA/SV; introduce and explain the formal methods used at each stage, stop short of full formal treatment, point to external sources.
           Core topic: production of digital goods; link traditional production economy to the digital economy of information and attention goods.

CONTENT    Arc: founder-led pricing (I Strategy: economic reasoning behind standard startup models, e.g. freemium, early bird) > formal policy under human control (II Policy) > autonomous pricing in the enterprise (III Engine).
           End product: a pricing engine, a function that takes environmental variables, simulates and tests pricing strategies, forms and tests conjectures about consumer and competitor behavior, segments the market, respects firm-policy and regulatory constraints, and outputs a real-time price vector plus pointers for evolving the portfolio.
           Constraints on purely selfish optimization are framed as integrity (value-chain integrity), not ethics: efficiency (all resources put to best use) and accountability (contributions traceable to contributors; the effort reflects their value) across a multi-step, multi-location, multi-contributor chain.
           Bounded rationality via the Carnegie School (Simon, Cyert, March, Williamson); behavioralism kept to the necessary minimum; machine learning as proxy for organizational learning and decision making.
           Bookshelf: Tirole, Cabral, Bain (IO); Fudenberg & Tirole, Myerson, Tadelis (game theory); Bolton & Dewatripont; Simon; March & Simon; Williamson; Penrose; Angrist & Pischke; Armstrong; Shapiro & Varian.
